HOW MATCHING WORKS

Not just the nearest. The nearest who can actually fix it.

A ride-hailing app sends the closest driver. A repair call needs more than that: the closest technician who has never seen this fault may take longer to fix it and make a second trip. Cognify matches on distance, verified experience, skill and availability, and shows every number.

The whole flow

From a report to a repair, in four steps

1

Someone reports it

A facility manager reports a problem in the app or on the web: site, unit, what is wrong, how urgent.

2

It is ranked

Every open problem gets a priority score, so the board shows what to handle first, not just what came first.

3

Notified like a ride, layer by layer

Everyone free in the nearest radius is notified at once, with 45 seconds to answer. The best match among those who accept gets the job. If everyone declines, the next radius opens.

4

Briefed and closed

They accept, drive over, get the four-agent briefing, and log the visit. What they log goes into the shared memory.

Why this beats "nearest"

Two ways to send a technician

Nearest driver (ride-hailing style)

Picks whoever is closest. A new joiner who has never seen this fault arrives first, guesses, and the unit trips again. A second trip costs a technician's day and an SLA penalty.

Cognify: close and proven

Distance still counts for the most, but a technician who has fixed this fault before, and whose fixes stayed fixed, is preferred. The experience is counted from the raw visit records, not self-reported.
