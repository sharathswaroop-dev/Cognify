# Sample SOP: water-cooled chiller, high head pressure trips

1. Log the condenser water inlet and outlet temperatures and the head pressure at the trip.
2. Look at the condenser approach temperature. A large approach with clean tower water points to scale in the tubes, not to refrigerant.
3. Do NOT top up refrigerant first. In the recorded cases a top-up held for under two weeks every time.
4. Check the cooling-tower fan and water treatment log. Poor treatment in monsoon season is the usual cause of scale.
5. If scale is confirmed, schedule a chemical descale and a flush of the tower loop. Record the approach temperature before and after.
