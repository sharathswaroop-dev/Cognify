# Case study (sample data): Daikin VRV IV, inverter PCB ordered twice

**Symptom:** E3 compressor-protection trip, unit not cooling.

**Tried first, did not fix it:** replacing the compressor contactor, then replacing the compressor relay board.

**What fixed it:** reseating a loose connector on the inverter board and updating the firmware. No new PCB was needed.

**Lesson:** before ordering an inverter PCB, reseat the connector and check board revision and firmware. Two of three PCB orders in this fleet were unnecessary.
