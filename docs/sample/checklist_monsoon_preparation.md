# Sample checklist: before the monsoon

1. Descale or wash condenser coils on water-cooled chillers; record the approach temperature.
2. Walk every drain line: slope, trap, no flat runs.
3. Check cooling-tower water treatment records.
4. Inspect rooftop unit weather seals and contactor housings for water ingress.
5. Make sure each van carries spare contactors and drain fittings.
