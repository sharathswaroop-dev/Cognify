# Handover note (sample data): Riverside Apartments, Block C

Written by a technician who is moving to another region.

- The rooftop condensing units here are on a weak supply. Voltage dips to the low 190s in the evening. Several "faulty compressor" calls were really supply problems.
- Drain lines on the indoor cassettes were laid without a trap. Two ceiling leaks came from that. Flush with nitrogen and re-lay with a slope and a trap.
- The building manager prefers visits before 11:00. Access to the roof needs the security desk key.
