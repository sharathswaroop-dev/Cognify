# Sample lessons learned: fixes that did not hold

- Topping up refrigerant on a chiller with scaled tubes: tripped again within two weeks.
- Replacing the compressor relay board on Daikin VRV IV E3 trips: never fixed the fault.
- Clearing a blocked drain without re-laying it with a slope and trap: leak returned.
- Replacing pump bearings for vibration caused by shaft misalignment: no change.
