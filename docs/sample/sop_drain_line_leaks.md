# Sample SOP: indoor unit leaking water

- Water on a false ceiling almost always means the drain line, not the coil.
- Check slope (minimum 1:100) and whether a trap was fitted. Lines laid flat with no trap were the cause in most recorded visits.
- Clearing the blockage alone brings the leak back. Flush with nitrogen, re-lay the pipe with a proper slope and fit a trap.
- Photograph the repaired run and add the photo to the job record.
