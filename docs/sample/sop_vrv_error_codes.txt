Sample SOP: Daikin VRV IV outdoor unit, error E3 (high-pressure / compressor protection trip)

1. Read the supply voltage at the unit under load. Below about 200 V, suspect the supply or the contactor before the compressor.
2. Inspect the compressor contactor coil for burn marks and the contacts for pitting. In this fleet the contactor was the root cause far more often than the compressor.
3. Do not replace the compressor relay board as a first step: on comparable units it replaced the fault without fixing it in every recorded case.
4. If the trip returns after a new contactor, reseat the inverter board connector before ordering a new inverter PCB, then check the board revision and firmware.
5. Record every attempt, including the ones that failed, so the next engineer does not repeat them.
