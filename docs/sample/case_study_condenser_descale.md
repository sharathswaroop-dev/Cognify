# Case study (sample data): Green Leaf Mall chiller, repeated high-pressure trips

**Equipment:** Carrier 30XW water-cooled chiller, Green Leaf Mall, Koramangala.
**Symptom:** Trips on high condenser pressure every few weeks, worst in the monsoon months.

## What was tried, and failed
- Recharging refrigerant twice. The trip came back within ten days each time.
- Washing the condenser tubes with water only. It held for about a month.
- Replacing the high-pressure switch. The switch was fine.

## What worked
Chemical descaling of the condenser tubes and flushing the cooling-tower water loop. Stable for twelve months after.

## Lesson
When the same chiller trips on high pressure across seasons, look at scale in the condenser tubes before touching refrigerant charge or switches.
